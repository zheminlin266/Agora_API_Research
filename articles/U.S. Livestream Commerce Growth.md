### Whatnot's Growth

Whatnot started with collectibles, scarce items, and other non-standard merchandise. It now combines three models: an interest-based community, live auctions, and end-to-end e-commerce fulfillment. The platform initially addressed challenges in collectibles trading such as authentication, price discovery, inventory turnover, and trust between buyers and sellers. It later expanded into mainstream consumer categories including apparel, beauty, electronics, and jewelry.
