#### Estimated Return on Investment

Assumptions:

- Total area of 75,588 square meters, with 70% treated as rentable space. Yes, this assumes that some below-ground space can also be leased as office space.
- 70% occupancy.
- Rent of RMB 3.5 per square meter per day. The asking rents above may not be achievable in the current market, and it is also uncertain whether below-ground space can be leased, so this analysis uses a relatively conservative rent assumption.

Under these assumptions, annual rental revenue would be approximately RMB 47.31 million. Operating costs have not been deducted for now.

The estimated $277 million total project cost is approximately RMB 1.88 billion. **Annual rental income would recover about 2.5% of the total investment.**

### Bank Financing Terms and Policy Benefits

Given the project's very low apparent return, it is worth asking whether the company received favorable terms from the government or other parties. The bank financing terms provide one possible explanation.

| Term | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Bank | Shanghai Pudong Development Bank |
| Maximum principal | RMB 1.27 billion |
| Interest rate | The People's Bank of China's Loan Prime Rate (LPR) less 95 basis points; at current rates, this implies an annual rate of approximately 2.05%–2.55% |
| Principal repayment begins | 2028 |
| Final maturity | 2043 at the latest |
| Guarantee | A separate subsidiary of the group provides the guarantee |
| Collateral | Land-use rights for the headquarters project |
| Loan balance at year-end 2025 | $80.42 million |
| Loan balance at the end of 2026 Q1 | Approximately $91.13 million |

The loan's main benefits appear to be:

- **LPR minus 95 basis points.** Compared with ordinary corporate financing priced at LPR or LPR plus a spread, this is a meaningful pricing concession.
- **A long principal grace period.** The loan was signed in 2023, but principal repayment does not begin until 2028, giving the company roughly five years focused primarily on drawdowns and construction.
- **A long tenor.** With final maturity no later than 2043, this is a long-term project loan that avoids concentrated repayment pressure during the early construction period.

![China's Loan Prime Rate chart: 1-year and over-five-year LPR](/articles/shanghai-headquarters/China_LPR_chart_en.svg)

Based on the direction of China's LPR, if rates move down again, the bank may eventually be paying Agora to borrow. That obviously is not how things work in the real world—but at least the project's interest expense could continue to decline.
